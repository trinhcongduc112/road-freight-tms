# Login to the system

The login page is the gateway to all features in Road Freight TMS. Your account is provisioned by your organization Admin.

## Open the login page

Open your browser and visit:

```
https://<your-company-domain>/login
```

You'll see the login screen:

![Login page](/img/screenshots/login-page.png)

## Login steps

### Step 1 — Enter email

Enter the email address your Admin registered for you.

:::warning Note
Email must match **exactly** the invited address. If you didn't get the invitation email, contact your Admin.
:::

### Step 2 — Enter password

Enter the password you set when accepting the invitation.

![Login form filled](/img/screenshots/login-form.png)

### Step 3 — Click "Login"

If correct, the system routes you to the **Home** page for your role:

- **IT Admin / Planner** → Dashboard overview
- **Dispatcher** → Live Monitoring page
- **Accountant** → Reports
- **Driver** → Mobile app (no web access)

## Forgot password

If you forgot your password, click **"Forgot password?"** below the form.

### Recovery flow

1. Enter your account email
2. Check your inbox (and Spam)
3. Click the link to set a new password
4. Link is valid for **30 minutes**

## Switch language

Toggle between **Tiếng Việt** and **English** with the button at the bottom-left of the sidebar.

## Sign out

Click your avatar in the top-right corner → choose **"Sign out"** to end your session.

:::tip Security
Always sign out when using a shared computer. The system auto-signs you out after **8 hours of inactivity**.
:::

## Common errors

| Error | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Email or password incorrect" | Wrong credentials | Check Caps Lock |
| "Account not verified" | Email not confirmed | Check inbox or contact Admin |
| "Account is disabled" | Disabled by Admin | Contact Admin |
| "Too many failed logins" | Rate limit | Wait 15 minutes |
